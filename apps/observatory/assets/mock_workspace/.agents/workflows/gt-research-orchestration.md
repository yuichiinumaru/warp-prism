# gt-research-orchestration

## Purpose

Orchestrate research tasks: gather external intelligence, perform competitive analysis, look up API documentation, and synthesize findings into actionable knowledge for the workspace.

## Steps

1. Receive research request with scope and objectives
2. Identify relevant external sources and tools
3. Execute research using agent-cli-delegation for tool access
4. Synthesize findings into structured report
5. Record findings in agent-wiki for continuity
6. Update related tasks with research evidence

## Skills Used

- `external-intelligence`: Research methodologies and source management
- `agent-wiki`: Persisting research findings for future reference
- `agent-cli-delegation`: Tool-based research execution

## Artifacts Produced

- Research report with findings
- Agent-wiki entries for knowledge continuity
- Task evidence updates
