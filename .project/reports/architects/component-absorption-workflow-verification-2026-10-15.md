## Design reasoning / canonical ownership

The generic donor lifecycle already owns source harvest, target comparison and
preservation. The local workflow delegates those methods by reference rather
than copying them, skips disposal assessment when not requested, and does not
apply donor disposal to owned-consumer inventory. Local bridge owns source
routing/coverage; the adaptation reference owns the project-specific questions;
mission/layer/visual/token owners keep their existing meaning. Five phases are
decision boundaries, not five mandatory artifacts or actors. This satisfies the
Principia reality, ontology, single-owner, progressive-disclosure, economy,
least-authority and independence concerns without a new framework or Rule.

The User's coverage intent is explicit: account for consumer components already
implemented and still needed, even before bridge inventories are complete.
Product-local dispositions retain the reusable presentation seam and remaining
unsupported need; they do not silently discard coverage. Donor suites/copies do
not count as independent consumers. EXPERIMENTAL study is not stable promotion.
